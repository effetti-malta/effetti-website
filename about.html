Our Story

From a vision
to Malta's fastest
growing AV company

Effetti was founded with a single ambition: to fill a gap in the local market for a truly complete, engineered AV solution — one that didn't require clients to piece together different vendors for sound, lighting, power and planning.

We set out to become Malta's one-stop AV partner. A company where the client walks in with a vision, and walks away having experienced exactly that — and then some. Since launching in 2024 we've grown at a pace that's made us one of the most talked-about AV companies on the island.

Behind every event is a team of qualified designers and warranted engineers — people who understand both the technical demands and the creative ambition behind every brief. We bring industry-grade equipment, deep technical know-how, and a genuine care for getting it right.

But what truly sets us apart is the relationship. We stay close to our clients — before, during, and long after the event is over. For us, every event is the beginning of an ongoing partnership, not a transaction.

Sound Engineering
Professional PA systems, mixing, speaker arrays and acoustic planning — tailored to every venue and audience size.
Lighting Design
Stage, ambient and effect lighting that transforms a space — from subtle corporate elegance to full festival-grade production.
Visual & AV Systems
Screens, projectors, LED walls and full AV integration — ensuring your message lands with maximum visual impact.
Power Distribution
Safe, reliable power infrastructure for any setup — planned and managed by warranted engineers so nothing is left to chance.
What drives us

Our core
principles.

The values that shape every decision, every setup, every relationship.

01
Client Peace of Mind
Your event is one of the most important moments for your audience. We take that seriously. From the first call to the final pack-down, we ensure you're never left worrying about the technical side — because we've got it handled.
02
True Customisation
We don't do cookie-cutter setups. Every event starts with a deep conversation about your vision, your space, your audience. We then engineer a solution built exactly around that — not a standard package forced to fit.
03
Reliability, Always
In live events, failure is not an option. We maintain our equipment to the highest standards, plan redundancy into our setups, and show up with qualified engineers who have seen it all — so the show always goes on.
04
Technical Excellence
Our team includes warranted engineers and experienced designers who understand the science behind great sound and light. We invest in the best equipment and the knowledge to use it at its full potential.
05
Lasting Partnerships
We don't vanish after the event. We follow up, we learn, we improve. The goal is always a long-term relationship — clients who trust us so completely they never have to look elsewhere for their AV needs.
06
Full-Service Thinking
From AV equipment and power distribution to event planning support and access to our network of trusted specialists — we think holistically about your event, covering every angle so nothing falls through the cracks.
