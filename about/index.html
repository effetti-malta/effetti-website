Who we are

Built for
the sound.

Effetti Malta was born in 2024 with a clear purpose: to bring engineered, fully customised AV solutions to events across Malta - no matter the scale. From intimate private gatherings to large-scale celebrations, we treat every setup as its own project. But beyond the technical side, our real purpose is to be the reliable long-term partner our clients can depend on - so that when event day comes, they can focus entirely on their event, with complete peace of mind.
